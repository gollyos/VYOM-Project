# Completed: on the flute

To play the flute effectively, focus on these core fundamentals:

*   **Embouchure:** Position the embouchure hole against your lower lip, covering about one-third of it. Your lips should be relaxed but firm at the corners, forming a small, centered aperture.
*   **Air Stream:** Direct a focused, steady stream of air across the hole—not into it. Think of it like blowing across the top of a glass bottle.
*   **Posture:** Keep your head up, shoulders relaxed, and your back straight. Hold the flute parallel to the floor to ensure proper breath support and hand positioning.
*   **Hand Position:** Place your fingers on the keys with a gentle curve, as if you are holding a small ball. Keep your fingers close to the keys at all times to improve speed and accuracy.
*   **Breath Support:** Use your diaphragm to control your air. A consistent, supported air stream is the key to a clear, resonant tone across all registers.
